# E-Commerce Power BI Analysis Dashboard

**Overview:**
A data analysis project evaluating the performance of an online retailer. The dataset covers 80 orders from 12 customers across 10 products, spanning from January to July 2025[cite: 1].

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
* **Total Revenue:** $7,564[cite: 1].
* **Total Profit:** $3,591 (Average profit margin: 47.5%)[cite: 1].
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $94.55[cite: 1].
* **Return Rate:** 22.5% of total orders (18 returns), resulting in a refund cost of $486[cite: 1].

**Key Business Insights:**
* The **Bags** category accounted for the highest revenue share (32%), whereas the **Home Office** category had the lowest profit margin (43.2%)[cite: 1].
* Orders with discounts (representing 24% of total orders) generated a profit margin of 42.8%, compared to 48.8% for full-price orders[cite: 1].
* Late deliveries negatively impacted performance, representing 25% of the total orders and putting 38% of the total revenue at risk[cite: 1].

**Technical Process:**
1. **Data Cleaning:** Processed 80 rows of data, removed errors, and calculated derived fields such as Total Cost and Profit[cite: 1].
2. **Data Modeling:** Designed a Star Schema connecting the fact table (Orders) with dimension tables (Customers, Products, Calendar)[cite: 1].
3. **DAX Calculations:** Created key measures for profit margins, average order value, and return rates[cite: 1].
